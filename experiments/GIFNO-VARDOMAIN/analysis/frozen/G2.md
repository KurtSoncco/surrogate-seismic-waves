# G2

Paper 2's headline is positive for the IID test (720 records, 24 Sobol groups) and the dipping test (90 records, 3 Sobol groups), and it is not a lateral-accuracy result. On both of those tests the P128n128 pooled mean g log-L1, and the upper end of the master bootstrap, lie below the center-column outcrop and below the 9-column Pretell geometric-mean outcrop in 0.4–2.5 Hz and in 2.5–10 Hz. The same point estimates also lie below g=0. The 0.1–0.4 Hz band is reported as absolute error and is not part of that claim. Three-layer is not in the positive sentence: its mid-band prior sits under the 0.0136 skill floor, about a third of records are harmed, and the realization floor exceeds the 1D error. Treasure Island below 0.4 Hz is not an accuracy result, and the scored-band gaps there are smaller than the 15 s record-length leakage. Manifold widths and the synthetic 1000 m and 1500 m windows are compatibility. The training strip is 500 m.

## Rule

A positive headline requires the P128n128 pooled mean g log-L1 on the IID test and on the dipping test to sit below both baseline (b) components, the center-column outcrop and the Pretell geometric-mean outcrop, on the mid band (0.4–2.5 Hz) and the high band (2.5–10 Hz). The master-bootstrap upper end is reported beside that comparison. The low band is stated separately and is not part of the positive test. Lateral accuracy is outside this gate. Treasure Island below 0.4 Hz is outside this gate. `gates.G2_POSITIVE_BANDS` is mid and high.

Decision code: `positive_in_family_0p4_10`.

## Ladder

Aggregation is one reduction per file. `ladder.csv` pools stations and resamples Sobol groups or section ids (`bootstrap_unit=master`, skill of the pooled mean). `ladder_treasure.csv` is one section. Its interval is `bootstrap_moving_block`, which returns the median, and the pooled mean is a separate column. Skill uses `gates.noise_floor_log_l1()` (the proposal 0.0136). Absolute log-L1 is reported where that floor suppresses skill.

IID, dipping, and three-layer test counts: {'iid': 720, 'ood_dipping': 90, 'ood_three_layer': 90, 'manifold': 37}. Every test record is scored at every labeled station (21 stations, 15 m). Manifold val and blind sections that the cache loads are scored at every station inside the margin. Troughs are absent from that cache (76 dropped). Manifold interiors are pad-cropped. Round-0 unpadded 2000 m sections are a status row and were not scored. `full_svlab.h5` was not scored.

## Baselines

Baseline (a) is g=0, the stored per-station prior. Baseline (b) is the center-column elastic outcrop from `matched_physics.outcrop_many` and a 9-column geometric mean from `section_1d.surface_over_outcrop`. The column index list is in the sidecar. Rigid-base arms were not read. Realization and POD numbers are the Phase 1 tables. The FE floor is unmeasured on every row. The headline ratio uses max of the measured floors only and is marked provisional because an unmeasured FE floor could change it. The ratio is omitted when that gap is not positive. On IID and dipping, in the mid band, the high band, and overall, the Phase 1 realization floor exceeds the 1D error, so those ratios are omitted. Dipping in the low band, manifold blind, and Treasure Island have a finite ratio with status `provisional_fe_unmeasured`. Per-width manifold rows have no width-specific POD floor and no realization floor.

## What the low band and the widths are

The measured 1D-convention floor in the audit is 0.00022779293958443574 on the high band, a lower bound on max(FE, 1D) because the FE floor was not measured. Halving the 15 s Treasure Island record moves 0.1–0.4 Hz by 0.563. Treasure Island low-band and overall rows are labeled not an accuracy result. Flat width rows and the synthetic 500/1000/1500 m context probe repeat the edge of a 500 m strip or describe pad-cropped sections. They are compatibility.

## Epoch 46

Epoch 46 is the stored `best_model.pt` (`val_score` 0.04987). The selection that stored it averages IID and manifold validation log-L1. Blind was not in that score. `history.csv` is absent, so this phase does not say epoch 46 is the minimum of a curve. Neighboring epochs 43–45 and 47–49 are not on disk and were not trained. The four-checkpoint mean is the stored IID and dipping center curves. Those curves were not recomputed. Three-layer, manifold, and Treasure Island have no stored ensemble spectra. Stage 1 has no spectra. No validation horse-race among the four checkpoints is stated.

## Consistency

Cutoffs are the proposals in `gates.py`: homogeneous median L1(|g_pred|) against 0.0136, mirror station-matched L1 against 0.1 times the median L1(g_true), monotone sign agreement 0.80. Monotone pairs were not run: existing records do not hold a pair that changes only CoV or only dip. CoV=0 uses `make_cov0_window` on every IID test record. Context invariance is the compatibility block.

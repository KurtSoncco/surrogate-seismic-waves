# Phase 1 audit — Gate G1

The measured noise floor of the stored training labels is **0.000228** log-L1 on 2.5–10 Hz, and smaller on the other bands (0.1–0.4 Hz 1.82e-5, 0.4–2.5 Hz 1.35e-4, 0.1–10 Hz 1.21e-4). That is the live 1D-convention term: the larger of the cache-versus-fixed-window median and a fresh `surface_over_outcrop` column residual. The finite-element floor was not measured, so this number is a lower bound on max(FE floor, 1D-convention uncertainty). It sits below the proposal placeholder **0.0136** on every band. `gates.py` was not edited. No test, validation, or manifold-blind median of L1(g_true) falls at or below this measured floor (`signal.csv`, `fraction_at_or_below_floor` is 0). The proposal would have treated the three-layer mid-band residual (median 0.00337) as absolute-error territory; the measurement does not.

Record length is a separate, larger label error and is what G1 uses to drop claims. Halving the 15 s Treasure Island record moves log|TF| by 0.563 (0.1–0.4 Hz), 0.042 (0.4–2.5 Hz), and 0.122 (2.5–10 Hz). Cutting one 50 s IID record to 15 s moves the low band by 0.395. Those figures are in `label_floors.csv` and `noise_floor.json`.

## 1.1 Census

The IID cache is 7680 records in 256 groups, split 6240/720/720, a 500 m strip of a 1500 m mesh with depth capped at 110 rows. Dipping and three-layer are 960 records each, split 780/90/90. The manifold cache holds 301 section npz files, split train/val/test/blind 250/35/0/16. The Box design has 312 rows and 301 complete; its `split` column is the design label (285/16), and training uses `manifold_holdout` instead. Every cached manifold section is a gradient soil fill. Box H5 counts match the caches (IID 7680, dipping 960, three-layer 960, manifold 301). `corner_is` has 170 H5s, is readable, and is not a cache family, so it was inventoried and not scored. Round-0 is 64 local unpadded 2000 m × 150 m sections, duration 15 s, with the absorbing boundary inside a 2000 m window. They are flagged and are not clean 2000 m domains. `full_qvs.h5` is the Treasure Island solve (Vs 150×4000, 15 s, 500 m pads on a 5000 m solve, Q–Vs damping). `full_svlab.h5` has the same Vs and the same surface acceleration; `Damping_zeta` differs by up to 0.027 and the stored base damping is the CSV pair 0.0075/0.00075. It is not a second solve.

## 1.2 Coverage

Dimensionless coordinates are in `coverage.csv` (9902 rows). Every IID and OOD training window is 500 m, so λ/W at 0.1 Hz on the IID training set occupies only 2.44–5.36 (5th–95th percentile). Treasure Island on the scored 2000 m window is 1.13, outside that strip. Manifold sections of 1500 m or wider have median λ/W at 0.1 Hz of 1.55. A low-frequency lateral question trained at 500 m sits where the training density has no other width.

## 1.3 Label floors

Points per wavelength at 10 Hz are computed from the meshes on disk at the slowest stored Vs. On the training families that minimum is about 57–68 m/s, which is 5.7–6.8 points per wavelength at dx = 1 m. Round-0 and Treasure Island are finer at their own minima (about 10 and 14) because those meshes are faster, not because a refined mesh was solved. No mesh-refinement study and no repeated OpenSees solve were launched. The FE log-L1 floor is **unmeasured** on every band. Pad contamination is deferred to the pilot. Round-0 stays flagged.

Record-length leakage was measured on existing files with the 1310.72 s FFT window. Treasure Island, 15 s against 7.5 s: 0.563 / 0.042 / 0.122 / 0.223 (low / mid / high / overall). One west crop, 60 s against 15 s: 0.024 / 0.0031 / 0.045 / 0.022. The same crop, 60 s against 30 s, moves the low band by 0.017. IID `run_0.h5` stores `recorders/accel/data` (base then surface, 21 and 21), not `recorders/accel/surface`; on the surface channels, 50 s against 15 s moves the low band by 0.395 and the mid band by 0.0059. One round-0 section, 15 s against 7.5 s, moves the low band by 0.150. The historical 1D engine is a different shift: round-0 versus round-1 fixed priors differ by 0.090 / 0.177 / 0.265, and the 2D transfer functions differ by about 0.20 on every band. Case 7 and SVLAB “versus 1D” numbers computed with the old engine are not comparable to the current cache.

## 1.4 Signal

L1(g_true) is the record-mean absolute column residual. Test medians, low / mid / high / overall: IID 0.0104 / 0.0996 / 0.205 / 0.107; dipping 0.00718 / 0.0780 / 0.172 / 0.0860; three-layer 0.000942 / 0.00337 / 0.102 / 0.0326. Manifold blind medians by width (n = 4 each) stay above the measured floor; the 2000 m blind low band is 0.00353 and the mid band is 0.0294. A 64-row center-station sample scored with a zero prediction matches `prior_log_l1` in the harness. Bootstrap intervals in `signal.csv` resample records.

## 1.5 Realization floor

Within a Sobol group the station-mean pairwise |g_i − g_j|, then the median across groups, is the OpenSees spread of a fixed θ. IID (256 groups): 0.00970 / 0.136 / 0.258 / 0.134. Dipping is similar in the mid band (0.137) and smaller in the low band (0.00371). Three-layer (32 groups): 0.000608 / 0.00824 / 0.188 / 0.0615. The three-layer mid-band seed spread (0.00824) exceeds the three-layer mid-band signal (0.00337). P128n128 sees the realization, so this spread is a ceiling for a θ-only model and a scale the three-layer mid-band correction does not clear.

## 1.6 Representation floors

`g_true` was projected with `pod_{modes,mean,weight}.npy` and reconstructed as mean + coeff @ modes / weight. That reconstruction matches `model.reconstruct_pod` to 1e-5 on a zero curve. Station-mean reconstruction error on the pooled test set keeps 44% of the low-band residual and 96% of the mid-band residual (signal 0.0985, floor 0.00355). Treasure Island keeps 1.4% of its low-band residual (signal 0.0706, floor 0.0696) and 91% of its mid-band residual. Depth PCA uses the 512-wide column code, interleaved log(Vs/400) and log(zeta/0.02), through `pca_components.npy` (128, 512) and `pca_mean.npy`. `physical_features` is four scalars per column and was stored beside the code; it was not multiplied by the 512-wide basis. Median reconstruction L1 of the column code: IID test 0.071, dipping test 0.095, three-layer test 0.165, manifold val 0.236, manifold blind 0.186, Treasure Island 0.231, against IID train 0.059. Pools are reported separately in `representation.csv`.

## 1.7 Split integrity

`split_by_group(group, seed=42)` reproduces the IID, dipping, and three-layer cache splits with zero mismatches and zero groups in two splits. `manifold_holdout(seed=42)` reproduces the 301 section npz splits with zero mismatches and zero `group_id` crossings. The status flag `fail` on seeds is a reused integer, not a duplicated sample: each of the 30 dipping and three-layer `rf_seed` values is the realization index inside every Sobol group, so each seed appears in train, val, and test; one IID `rf_seed` is shared by groups 125 (train) and 128 (test); manifold `field_seed` 0 labels 32 different sections spread across train, val, and blind. Within-IID adversarial AUC is 0.48. Dipping, three-layer, and the manifold pool separate from IID train at AUC 1.00, 1.00, and 0.9996, which is the designed shift. Manifold train versus val is 0.58. Manifold train versus blind is 0.77 on 16 sections. Nearest-neighbor distance in the same PCA-plus-scalar space has Spearman 0.44 (IID) and 0.39 (dipping) against center-station overall error. Treasure Island is farther from the IID training cloud than every synthetic test point in that space.

Epoch 46 is the stored checkpoint. `val_score` is 0.04987 and `iid_log_l1` is 0.05157. `select_families` is `iid,manifold`: the mean of IID and manifold validation log-L1, query dtype float32, amp on, agreement under 0.0136. Blind is not in `select_families`. `train.selection_score` for a unified stage is manifold validation only; this checkpoint took the p128 branch instead. `history.csv` is not in the checkpoint directory, so the claim that epoch 46 is the minimum of the logged curve was not recomputed. The stored score matches the paper’s 0.0499.

## 1.8 Frozen P128n128 errors

IID and dipping are the run_g0 center-station tables (one station per record). Three-layer test (21 stations, 500 m window) and the manifold sections the cache actually loads were scored with `load_fp32` only. Troughs are absent from that cache: 26 of 35 val sections and 11 of 16 blind sections were forwarded. Treasure Island is the existing `treasure_long.csv` aggregated at record level (200 stations). No second dtype was loaded and the checkpoint was not fine-tuned.

Pooled skill, which is the skill of the mean log-L1, on 0.4–2.5 Hz: IID center 0.549 (log-L1 0.0520), dipping center 0.560 (0.0352), three-layer 0.110 (0.00310), manifold val 0.245 (n = 26), manifold blind 0.437 (n = 11), Treasure Island 0.057 (0.0441 against a column 0.0468). The three-layer mid-band figure matches the paper’s 0.00310 and 0.110. Harness harm rate on that band is 0.32 of three-layer test records, 0.040 of IID center stations, and 0.056 of dipping center stations. Overall error rises with CoV on IID (Spearman 0.50) and with impedance on dipping (0.77). The twenty worst IID center-station records cluster in run indices 7354–7379. Manifold blind mid-band errors by width use 2–4 sections each.

## Claim map

Each Paper 2 sentence is tied to the audit item that bears on it.

**Keep, with the station set named.** Horizontal mid-band skill 0.548 and log-L1 0.0513 (1.4, 1.8). The audit’s center-station pool is 0.549 and 0.0520, above the measured floor and above the 50-versus-15 mid-band leakage of 0.0059. Dipping mid-band skill is the same kind of result; the audit’s center-station pool is 0.560 against the paper’s all-station 0.536. Three-layer mid-band log-L1 0.00310 and pooled skill 0.110 (1.4, 1.8) sit above the measured floor. The absolute gap is smaller than the three-layer seed spread in 1.5, and 32% of those records are harmed, so the sentence that belongs with 0.110 is the small residual, which the paper already states.

**Rescope to compatibility.** The flat width skills at 500/1000/1500 m (horizontal 0.548/0.544/0.543, dipping 0.536/0.530/0.527, three-layer 0.110/0.123/0.117) describe a 500 m training strip placed in a wider window (1.2). They are not a low-frequency lateral result. The paper’s “(trained)” label on the 1000 m and 1500 m horizontal rows is that compatibility evaluation.

**Rescope the metric.** Practice-box Pearson values (horizontal 0.979, dipping 0.986, Pretell 0.946 and 0.970) are linear |TF|. The harness Pearson is log|TF|. Rankings that survive are the log-L1 rankings on bands whose signal clears the measured floor and the record-length leakage. The paper’s harm rates (Pearson or Anderson worse than the column, 4.6% and 2.6% on horizontal) are a different predicate from harness `harm_rate` (1.8).

**Rescope the chained stage sentence.** “0.616 → 0.556 → 0.548” and the three-layer bar −5.689 mix mean-of-skills with pooled skill (G0, 1.4). Stage-2 three-layer log-L1 0.0137 sits on the 0.0136 proposal, which this audit did not adopt. The measured floor does not suppress the P128n128 three-layer skill. The four-checkpoint mean skill 0.471 is an appendix average on the same small residual and was not re-scored.

**Rescope Treasure Island mid-band skill.** Median skill 0.093 and the log-L1 gap 0.0304 versus 0.0321 (1.3, 1.8). The audit’s pooled mid-band gap on the 200 stations is 0.0027. Halving the 15 s record moves that band by 0.042, and cutting a 60 s west record to 15 s moves it by 0.003. The quoted improvement is at the scale of the record length.

**Rescope the manifold width curve and the blind skill 0.275.** Items 1.2, 1.6, 1.7, and 1.8. Blind non-trough n is 11, and each width has 2–4 sections. The audit’s pooled mid-band skill on those 11 is 0.437, on a set a logistic classifier separates from manifold train at AUC 0.77. Depth-PCA reconstruction of manifold columns (0.19–0.24) is several times the IID training reconstruction (0.059). The published per-width skills (0.451, 0.209, 0.201, 0.278) are not a resolved width result.

**Rescope epoch 46.** The paper says epoch 46 is the lowest validation score, falling to 0.0499 (1.7). The stored `val_score` is 0.04987. The selection that produced it averages IID and manifold validation log-L1. Blind is logged and is outside the score. The learning-curve minimum was not recomputed because `history.csv` is absent.

The low-band residual is 0.071, the 128-mode basis keeps 1.4% of it, the model’s pooled skill is 0.007, and halving the 15 s record moves the band by 0.563. Any accuracy claim that treats round-0 unpadded 2000 m sections as clean domains (1.1, 1.3). The SVLAB appendix median Pearson 0.881 versus 0.869 (1.1): `full_svlab.h5` is the same surface motion as `full_qvs.h5`, so it is not an independent damping experiment, and that Pearson is linear |TF| on 0.1–2.5 Hz.

The FE numerical floor, on every band. Pad contamination, deferred to the pilot. A record longer than 50 s, so the IID low band was not compared with a window longer than the training record; the 50-versus-15 comparison was measured. The epoch-46 minimum along `history.csv`. `corner_is` scores. Trough sections, which the cache does not load. All-station IID and dipping forwards; those two families use the center station already written by run_g0. No Savio or Stampede job was submitted, and `gates.py` was not overwritten with a floor fit to this audit.
